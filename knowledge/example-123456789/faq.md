# MyDeFi Protocol FAQ

## What is MyDeFi Protocol?
MyDeFi Protocol is a decentralized lending platform built on Base. Users can supply assets to earn yield or borrow against their collateral.

## How do I get started?
1. Connect your wallet at app.mydefi.xyz
2. Approve the assets you want to supply
3. Deposit collateral to start earning

## What are the supported assets?
Currently, we support ETH, USDC, USDT, and WBTC.

## Where can I find the documentation?
Visit docs.mydefi.xyz for full technical documentation.

## How do I contact support?
Open a ticket in the #support channel or email support@mydefi.xyz
